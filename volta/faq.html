Questions people actually ask

The shield

What does the shield actually block

A contactless card has no battery. A reader held close enough powers it through the air and then reads it back. The copper and nylon mesh inside the card wall absorbs and reflects that field, so the card never receives enough power to answer.

It covers the 13.56 MHz band that bank cards and biometric passports use, and the 125 kHz band used by older office fobs and hotel keys.

How do you test it

Every production batch is checked on a bench reader at the workshop. A card is read on its own to confirm the reader works, then read again inside a closed wallet from the same batch. If the second read returns anything, the batch does not ship.

You can repeat this at any shop counter. Try to pay with the card inside the closed wallet. Nothing should happen.

Does it stop working over time

The mesh is a physical layer, not a coating, and it is bonded between the lining and the shell where nothing rubs against it. It does not wear out with use. If the leather itself splits far enough to expose the lining, that is a warranty repair and we will fix it.

Will my card still work through the wallet

No, and that is the point. Take the card out to pay. If you need one card to stay readable for a transit pass, keep it in an outside pocket rather than in the wallet.

Sizing

Which shape should I pick

Count what you carry. Five cards or fewer, take the Halden Cardholder. Six to eight cards plus notes, the Meridian Bifold. More than eight, or coins and receipts you want contained, the Sever Zip Wallet.

There are only three, so the comparison on the home page puts them side by side on capacity, weight and size.

Do the slots stretch

Yes, by design. Full-grain leather gives about half a millimetre per slot in the first month and then stops. A wallet that feels tight in week one is usually right by week four. A wallet that feels loose on day one will stay loose.

What is the difference between smooth and pebbled

Same hide, different finishing. Smooth is milled flat and matte, and it shows a patina faster. Pebbled is pressed with a grain that hides scuffs, which is the better choice for a back pocket or a bag with keys in it.

Orders and delivery

When will it arrive

Free EU shipping over €80. Below that it is €4,90, tracked.

Can I change or cancel an order

Until it ships, yes. Email the workshop with your order number and we will catch it if it is still on the bench. Once tracking is issued, treat it as a return instead.

Do you ship outside the EU

Yes, to the UK, Switzerland, Norway, the United States and Canada. Import duty and local tax are payable on delivery and are not included in the price shown here.

Is there gift wrapping

Every wallet ships in a rigid box with a cotton dust bag, which is presentable as it is. Add a note at checkout and we will leave the invoice out of the parcel and email it to you instead.

Care and warranty

How do I look after the leather

Less than you think. Wipe it with a dry cloth. Vegetable-tanned leather takes oil from your hands and darkens on its own, which is the finish most people are hoping for.

If it gets soaked, let it dry away from heat. A radiator will make it brittle. Conditioner once a year is plenty, and only if it looks dry.

What does the warranty cover

Two years on stitching and hardware. If a seam opens or a zip fails, send it back and we repair it, or replace the wallet if repair is not sensible. Scratches, darkening and softening are not faults, they are the leather doing what it does.

Details are in the shipping and returns policy.

Can I return a wallet I have used

Within thirty days, yes, worn or not, as long as it comes back in one piece. We pay return postage inside the EU. Carrying it for a fortnight is the only honest way to know whether the shape suits you.
