The material

One hide, two colourways, no corrected grain.

Every wallet on this page is cut from vegetable-tanned full-grain, the top layer of the hide with the natural grain left intact. It is the expensive part of the skin and the only part that patinas instead of peeling. Onyx and Navy are the same leather taken to two different drums.

The copper and nylon mesh goes in before the wallet is closed, bonded into the card walls rather than dropped in as an insert. You cannot remove it, and it cannot shift out of position.

How the shield works

Living with it

Full-grain does not need much. Most of what shortens a wallet's life is treatment it never asked for.

A dry cloth is enough

Wipe it down when it looks dull. Saddle soap and conditioners are built for tack, not for a wallet that already sits against you every day.

It will darken

Oils from your hands do the work. Navy shifts faster and further than Onyx. Both settle after roughly three months and then stay put.

Keep it off the radiator

Sustained direct heat is the one thing that genuinely damages full-grain. It dries the fibres and they do not come back.

Do not overfill it

The slots are cut to a card each. Doubling up stretches the mouth permanently, and a stretched slot never grips again.

Why only three

Every wallet here has to earn its place against the other two. If a new shape does not do something the Meridian, the Halden and the Sever cannot, we do not make it.
